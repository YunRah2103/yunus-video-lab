# Packet Guy V1 — Locked Character Spec

## Identity
- British-Bangladeshi / South Asian young male mascot
- Light-brown skin
- Messy black hair
- Light scruffy stubble
- Black cap with cyan chevron mark
- Oversized black hoodie
- Dark cargos
- White/grey sneakers with restrained cyan details

## Personality
- Curious
- Slightly tired
- Slightly sarcastic / dry humour
- Enthusiastic about tech without sounding corporate
- Reacts visibly when systems fail

## Palette
- Background: #070C11
- Charcoal: #1A1A1A
- Cyan accent: #00E5FF
- Off-white text: #F1F4F6
- Light-brown skin reference: #B85E3C family; keep natural rather than orange

## Hard consistency rules
1. Do not change ethnicity or skin tone.
2. Do not redesign clothing between episodes.
3. Cyan is the signature accent.
4. Keep the silhouette slim, casual, slightly slouched.
5. Keep expressions readable and a little exaggerated.
6. Do not generate a fresh unrelated mascot per video.
7. High-detail reference art may be simplified for animation, but proportions and identity stay fixed.

## Voice / writing rule
Packet Guy should explain complex systems plainly and occasionally make dry observations. Avoid generic documentary narration.
